About CAS

Creativity, Activity, Service - The heart of the IB Diploma Programme

What is CAS?

CAS (Creativity, Activity, Service) is one of the fundamental components of the IB Diploma Programme. It enables students to gain experience in personal development, creativity, and community service areas alongside their academic studies.

This program aims for students to acquire not only academic success but also character development, social responsibility, and lifelong learning skills.

CAS Learning Outcomes

1

Identify Strengths and Areas for Growth

Students identify their own strengths and areas for growth, and plan how to develop them.

2

Undertake Challenges

Students take risks to develop new skills and overcome challenges.

3

Plan and Initiate

Students demonstrate skills in planning, organizing, and initiating activities.

4

Show Commitment and Perseverance

Students show commitment and perseverance when facing challenges and overcoming obstacles.

5

Collaboration Skills

Students develop skills to work effectively with others.

6

Global Significance

Students develop awareness of and take action on issues of global significance.

7

Ethical Thinking

Students consider the ethical dimensions of their actions and behave responsibly.
